A genuine rolling walk-forward evaluation (never a random or k-fold split) found the complex hybrid model's directional accuracy is statistically indistinguishable from a coin flip, and a Diebold-Mariano significance test shows it is measurably worse than a simple econometric ARIMA baseline (p < 0.001). This is consistent with market micro-efficiency at the one-day horizon for a heavily-traded index — not a bug to be "fixed" by tuning harder. Re-validated on 19 months of additional real data extending the evaluation window forward — the finding held, the significance sharpened.
